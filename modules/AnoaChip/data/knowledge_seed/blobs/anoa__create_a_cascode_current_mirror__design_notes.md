# Cascode Current Mirror — Build Contract

## 1. Specification
- **Topology class**: Cascode current mirror (NMOS sink, single output)
- **PDK**: gf180mcuD (GlobalFoundries 180 nm)
- **Device models**: gf180mcu_fd_pr__nfet_03v3, gf180mcu_fd_pr__pfet_03v3
- **Supply**: VDD = 3.3 V
- **Input current**: 10 µA (reference current to mirror)
- **Mirror ratio**: 1:1
- **Output current**: 10 µA
- **Output resistance**: ≥ 10 MΩ (cascode-boosted)
- **Output compliance**: ≥ 1.0 V (minimum output voltage for regulated current)
- **DC gain (Rout)**: ≥ 140 dB (intrinsic, unloaded)
- **Bandwidth**: ≥ 1 MHz (small-signal current transfer)
- **Unity-gain bandwidth**: ≥ 10 MHz
- **Phase margin**: ≥ 60° (current-mode transfer)
- **Power**: 33 µW (I_ref × VDD)
- **Load**: 1 pF (parasitic + probe capacitance at output node)

## 1b. Use case
**Biasing a precision bandgap reference in a temperature-sensor SoC.** A 10 µA reference current generated by a beta-multiplier core is mirrored into a bandgap's resistor ladder. The input is a DC 10 µA from the reference core; the expected output is a constant 10 µA sink across output node voltages from 1.0 V to 3.0 V. The application tolerates ≤ 1% current mismatch (100 nA error) over the compliance range, because a 1% current error translates to ~0.6°C temperature reading error in a first-order bandgap. The testbench sweeps V_out from 0 to 3.3 V and measures I_out flatness; a transient run injects a 100 mV step on the output node to verify settling < 1 µs into the 1 pF load.

## 2. Key Knowledge & Decisions

### Topology choice
A **single-output NMOS cascode current mirror** is selected. The simple two-transistor Wilson mirror is rejected because its output impedance (~gm·r_o·r_o) is insufficient and its input impedance is high, loading the reference core. The cascode mirror stacks a common-gate NMOS (M3/M4) above the diode/mirror pair (M1/M2), boosting output resistance by the intrinsic gain factor (g_m·r_o) of the cascode device. This achieves ≥ 10 MΩ Rout with a single stack, staying within the 3.3 V headroom (two V_dsat drops ≈ 0.4 V, leaving > 2.9 V compliance from VDD). A folded cascode is unnecessary here because we have a single supply and no high-swing-low-voltage constraint below 1 V.

### Sizing math
Square-law first-cut sizing, targeting moderate inversion (V_ov ≈ 200 mV) for good matching and reasonable bandwidth:

- **Mirror devices M1, M2**: Diode-connected M1 sets V_GS = V_th + V_ov. For I_ref = 10 µA, V_ov = 200 mV. Using gf180mcuD 3.3 V NMOS (µ_n·C_ox ≈ 100 µA/V², V_th ≈ 0.7 V):
  - W/L = 2·I / (µ_n·C_ox·V_ov²) = 2·10µ / (100µ·0.04) = 5 → W = 5 µm, L = 1 µm
  - L = 1 µm chosen to minimize channel-length modulation (λ), critical for mirror accuracy. Shorter L would improve bandwidth but degrade matching and Rout.
- **Cascode devices M3, M4**: Same W/L as mirror devices for matching. V_ov_cascode ≈ 200 mV. Cascode gate bias V_bias = V_th + V_ov + V_ov = 1.1 V (approximate; set by bias branch).
  - V_ds_M2 = V_ov ≈ 200 mV, keeping M2 at the edge of saturation with maximum headroom for M4.
- **Bias branch M5, M6**: Diode-connected pair generating V_bias_cascode. M5 mirrors I_ref (same W/L as M1), M6 is diode-connected cascode (same W/L as M3) stacked above M5. This self-biases the cascode gate at 2·V_ov + V_th above VSS.
- **Output resistance**: R_out ≈ (g_m4 · r_o4) · r_o2. With g_m4 ≈ √(2·µ_n·C_ox·(W/L)·I) ≈ 100 µS, r_o ≈ 1/(λ·I) ≈ 1/(0.05·10µ) ≈ 2 MΩ: R_out ≈ 100 µS × 2 MΩ × 2 MΩ = 400 MΩ ≫ 10 MΩ target. Margin absorbs layout parasitics and process variation.
- **Bandwidth**: Dominant pole at output node: f_p = 1/(2π·R_out·C_L) ≈ 1/(2π·400M·1p) ≈ 400 Hz. The current-transfer unity-gain frequency is set by g_m4/C_L ≈ 100µ/1p ≈ 16 MHz. The mirror's current-copying bandwidth (input to output) is limited by the diode node capacitance: f_3dB ≈ g_m1/(2π·C_g1) ≈ 100µ/(2π·~50f) ≈ 300 MHz. The spec bandwidth of 1 MHz is met with margin.

### Operating point
- **M1**: Diode-connected, saturation, V_GS = V_th + V_ov ≈ 0.9 V, V_ds = 0.9 V, I_d = 10 µA
- **M2**: Saturation, V_GS = 0.9 V, V_ds ≈ 0.2 V (set by cascode), I_d = 10 µA
- **M3**: Diode-connected cascode in bias branch, saturation, V_GS ≈ 0.2 V (overdrive only, since source sits at M5's drain ≈ 0.2 V), V_ds ≈ 0.2 V
- **M4**: Saturation (common-gate), V_GS ≈ 0.2 V, V_ds ≈ V_out - 0.2 V, I_d = 10 µA
- **M5**: Diode-connected, saturation, V_GS ≈ 0.9 V, I_d = 10 µA
- **M6**: Diode-connected, saturation, V_GS ≈ 0.2 V, I_d = 10 µA
- **Output compliance**: V_out_min ≈ V_ov_M2 + V_ov_M4 ≈ 0.4 V (ideal); spec requires ≥ 1.0 V, met with margin
- **Output swing**: 0.4 V to 3.3 V (limited by VDD on the high end, cascode saturation on the low end)

### Layout plan
- **Matching-critical pairs**: M1–M2 (mirror pair) and M3–M4 (cascode pair) are matching-critical. Interdigitate or place adjacent with shared centroid in a later iteration. M5–M6 (bias branch) match M1–M3 respectively for tracking.
- **Placement**: Input branch (M1, M3) on the left; output branch (M2, M4) on the right; bias branch (M5, M6) above or below the main pair. All devices share a common VSS rail at the bottom.
  - M1: center at (0, 0), M2: movex +10 µm (right of M1)
  - M3: center above M1 at (0, +5 µm), M4: center above M2 at (+10, +5 µm)
  - M5: movey -10 µm (below M1), M6: above M5 at (0, -5 µm)
- **Routes**:
  - `VSS`: straight_route along bottom, wide metal (carries no current but for substrate uniformity)
  - `V_bias_mirror` (gate of M1/M2, diode of M1): c_route from M1 drain/gate to M2 gate, short distance
  - `V_bias_cascode` (gate of M3/M4, diode of M6): c_route from M6 drain/gate to M3 gate and M4 gate, longer path — use via_stack to cross over
  - `I_in` (input current node, drain of M3): c_route from top of M3 to left pad
  - `I_out` (output node, drain of M4): c_route from top of M4 to right pad
  - `VDD`: not needed on-chip (current source external), but bias branch connects to I_in
- **Finishing order**: BARE devices (M1–M6, no ties/taps/dummies) → route all nets → LVS clean → add substrate ties (p+ tap ring around NMOS) → guard ring → dummies for density.

### Key techniques
1. **Self-biased cascode gate**: M5/M6 generate V_bias_cascode from the same I_ref, ensuring the cascode bias tracks process and temperature variations with the mirror. No external bias voltage needed.
2. **Equal W/L across all devices**: All six transistors share W = 5 µm, L = 1 µm, M = 1. This maximizes matching by making all devices identical; any lithographic or etch bias cancels in the ratio.
3. **L = 1 µm (not minimum)**: The 180 nm process allows L down to 180 nm, but L = 1 µm is chosen to reduce λ by ~5×, directly improving both mirror accuracy (ΔI/I due to V_ds mismatch) and output resistance. The bandwidth cost is negligible for a 1 MHz target.
4. **Single-supply operation**: The entire mirror operates between VDD = 3.3 V and VSS = 0 V. No charge pump or level shifter needed. The bias branch draws an additional 10 µA (total power = 2 × 10 µA × 3.3 V = 66 µW if bias branch is on-chip; 33 µW if only the mirror branch counts per spec).

## 4. Interfaces

```spice
.subckt cascode_current_mirror VDD VSS I_in I_out
* VDD   — positive supply (3.3 V)
* VSS   — ground / negative supply (0 V)
* I_in  — input reference current node (10 µA sink)
* I_out — mirrored output current node (10 µA sink)

.subckt mirror_core VSS V_bias_mirror I_in_cas I_out_cas
* VSS           — ground rail
* V_bias_mirror — gate bias net (diode-connected M1 drives M2 gate)
* I_in_cas      — source of input cascode (drain of M1)
* I_out_cas     — source of output cascode (drain of M2)

.subckt cascode_stack V_bias_cascode I_in I_out I_in_cas I_out_cas
* V_bias_cascode — gate bias for cascode devices (from bias_branch)
* I_in           — top-level input current node (drain of M3)
* I_out          — top-level output current node (drain of M4)
* I_in_cas       — source of M3 (connects to drain of M1)
* I_out_cas      — source of M4 (connects to drain of M2)

.subckt bias_branch VSS I_ref_bias V_bias_cascode
* VSS             — ground rail
* I_ref_bias      — input reference current for bias branch (10 µA)
* V_bias_cascode  — generated cascode gate bias voltage (~1.1 V)
```

## 5. Connections
- **M1** (diode NMOS): gate → drain → `V_bias_mirror`; source → `VSS`; drain → `I_in_cas`
- **M2** (mirror NMOS): gate → `V_bias_mirror`; source → `VSS`; drain → `I_out_cas`
- **M3** (input cascode NMOS): gate → `V_bias_cascode`; source → `I_in_cas`; drain → `I_in`
- **M4** (output cascode NMOS): gate → `V_bias_cascode`; source → `I_out_cas`; drain → `I_out`
- **M5** (bias diode NMOS): gate → drain → `I_ref_bias`; source → `VSS`; drain → `I_ref_bias`
- **M6** (bias cascode diode NMOS): gate → drain → `V_bias_cascode`; source → `I_ref_bias`; drain → `V_bias_cascode`
- **I_ref_bias** ties to **I_in** (same reference current feeds both mirror input and bias branch)
- **VDD** is the supply rail for the external current source; not connected to any NMOS drain in this NMOS-only mirror

```
VDD ──[I_source]── I_in ──┬── I_ref_bias ──┐
                         │                │
                    M3 drain          M5 gate/drain
                    M3 source ── M1 drain/gate ── M2 gate
                    (I_in_cas)    (V_bias_mirror)
                         │                │
                    M3 gate ── M4 gate ── M6 gate/drain
                    (V_bias_cascode)      │
                                    M6 source ── M5 drain
                                    (I_ref_bias)
                         │
                    M4 drain ── I_out (to load, 1 pF to VSS)
                    M4 source ── M2 drain
                    (I_out_cas)
                         │
                    M1, M2, M5 sources ── VSS
```

## 6. Verification & GDS Sign-off
- **`.op` sanity check**: Verify all six MOSFETs in saturation (V_ds > V_gs - V_th), I_d = 10 µA in each branch, V_bias_mirror ≈ 0.9 V, V_bias_cascode ≈ 1.1 V. Export operating point with `wrdata`.
- **`.dc` sweep**: Sweep V_out from 0 to 3.3 V at I_in = 10 µA. Measure I_out flatness: ΔI_out < 100 nA over V_out ∈ [1.0, 3.0] V. Extract R_out = dV_out/dI_out at V_out = 1.5 V; target ≥ 10 MΩ.
- **`.ac` analysis**: Inject small-signal current at I_in, measure current transfer I_out/I_in. Verify gain = 0 dB (1:1), bandwidth ≥ 1 MHz, unity-gain frequency ≥ 10 MHz, phase margin ≥ 60° at the unity-gain crossing. Export with `wrdata`.
- **`.tran` use-case**: Apply 10 µA DC input with a 100 mV step on V_out at t = 1 µs. Measure settling time to < 1% (100 nA) of final I_out; target < 1 µs into 1 pF load. Export transient data.
- **`.measure` lines**: `measure_dc_iout_flat`, `measure_rout`, `measure_ac_gain`, `measure_ac_bw`, `measure_ac_ugbw`, `measure_ac_pm`, `measure_tran_settling`.
- **Path to silicon on gf180mcuD**:
  1. Generate JSON config from parameter table → `glayout build` places bare devices and routes all nets.
  2. **DRC** (magic) against gf180mcuD rules: fix spacing, width, antenna violations.
  3. **LVS** (netgen): verify schematic vs extracted layout netlist match — all six devices, all nets connected per Section 5.
  4. **PEX** (magic + netgen): extract parasitic capacitances and resistances, re-run `.op`/`.dc`/`.ac`/`.tran` on extracted netlist.
  5. Add substrate ties (p+ tap ring), guard ring (p-substrate guard ring around NMOS block), and dummy devices for density matching.
  6. Re-run DRC + LVS on final layout with ties/ring/dummies.
  7. Export **GDSII** for gf180mcuD tape-out.
